# Touch Controls

On a touchscreen, you can edit the drawing directly or use the loupe for more
precise placement. These controls work in the browser and desktop editor.

## Direct touch

- Tap to select or place an object with the active tool.
- Drag with one finger to move objects or make a selection, as with the mouse.
- Hold a finger still for about a second to open the drawing context menu.
- Move two fingers together to pan the drawing; pinch them apart or together to
  zoom the canvas around the gesture.
- Drag a symbol preview from the library panel onto the drawing to place it.

## Precise placement with the loupe

The loupe magnifies the area around an aiming crosshair, so your finger does not
cover the connection point you are trying to reach.

1. Tap the drawing if necessary to reveal **Show Loupe**, then tap that button
   at the bottom of the drawing.
2. Slide one finger across the canvas to aim the crosshair. Movement is slower
   than your finger for finer control; lifting your finger leaves the aim in place.
3. Use the labeled controls below the lens:

   | Control | Action |
   |---|---|
   | **Click** | Click at the crosshair to select an object or place a point with the active tool. |
   | **Drag** | Begin dragging at the crosshair. Slide a finger across the canvas to move the pointer. |
   | **Drop** | Finish the drag. This replaces **Drag** while a drag is active. |
   | **Menu** | Open the context menu at the crosshair. |
   | **Close** | Close the loupe and return to direct touch. |

4. Tap **Hide Loupe** to return to direct touch.

![Touch loupe showing a magnified connection area, crosshair, and Click, Drag and Menu controls.](../images/touch-loupe.png)

For example, to draw a wire, choose **Wire**, aim at the starting pin, and tap
**Click**. Slide your finger to aim at the next point and tap **Click** again.
Use **Menu** for the tool's context commands when you want to finish or cancel.

Two-finger pan and pinch zoom also work with the loupe open. Finish an active
drag with **Drop** before changing the view.
